Philosophy

Everyone is fast now. Almost no one is deliberate. That gap is where I work.

Most editors start on the timeline. I start on paper. Nine years cutting video and six of them getting paid for it taught me that the edit is never the hard part — deciding what the piece has to do is. Hook, tension, payoff. Everything after that is execution.

Retention isn't luck, it's structure. A brand isn't a logo, it's a set of rules that still holds on the hundredth post. That's why I don't edit by eye and why nothing here was improvised — a system is what lets you improvise without getting lost.

I write it, board it, shoot it, cut it, design it and code it. One person across the whole chain means nothing gets lost in a handoff and nothing waits on someone else's calendar. Agencies take weeks. I deliver in days — and it still looks like it was made on purpose.

Currently: short-form systems, motion design, and interfaces that ship.
